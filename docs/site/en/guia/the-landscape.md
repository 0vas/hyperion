# A landscape for your AI’s work

When AI stops only answering and starts acting, you need a landscape — not only a chat.

Hyperion turns a request into a visible process: the agent runs what is ready; you decide; the evidence remains.

## Before: chat alone

For years AI was mostly consultative: questions, answers and a lengthening thread. Useful for thinking; fragile when work crosses tools, turns and people.

## Now: execution in view

Agents, MCP and tools let AI act. Hyperion does not replace your chat: it adds the landscape where you see the plan, human gates and what is already recorded.

On the [home page](/en/) you can scrub the scanner: one card crosses from chat into a Hyperion flow. This page explains the landscape in words — the agent runs what is ready; you decide; the evidence remains.

## From consulting to executing

When work runs long, the transcript stops being a map. You lose the order, who should act and what is already done.

You need a shared process — provider-independent — that keeps state, roles and public proof of progress.

## What the landscape holds

### Visible plan

From the conversation comes a path of jobs and steps. You see what is ready, in progress or waiting.

### Human decisions

Approvals and answers are yours. Hyperion does not invent consent or complete manual tasks for you.

### Real evidence

Logs, outputs and artifacts record what happened. An animation or an SSE alone does not prove an agent is executing.

## You in the loop

The agent prepares and runs what is eligible. When the process needs a decision, the canvas waits with you. On confirm, work can continue with what you declared.

## Day to day

1. You state the goal in your AI chat.
2. You open the canvas and follow the process.
3. You answer when Hyperion asks.
4. You continue from recorded state — not from thread memory alone.
